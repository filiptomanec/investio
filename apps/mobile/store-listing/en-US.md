## Description

<!-- Target: 4000 chars max. Written for scan-reading: hook → bullets → disclaimer. -->

**What does compound interest actually do to your money over 20 years? Find out in under a minute.**

Investio is a compound-interest calculator built for anyone who wants to understand their investments — not just look them up. Enter a starting deposit, a monthly contribution, and an annual return, and Investio shows you how your money grows year by year: nominal value, real purchasing power after inflation, projected passive income, and a full portfolio breakdown by milestone year.

Everything runs on your device. No account required. No data collected. No internet connection needed.

**What you can calculate:**

- Future portfolio value (nominal and inflation-adjusted)
- Real purchasing power — what your money will actually be worth in today's prices
- Monthly passive income from your portfolio using the 4 % rule (customizable withdrawal rate 0–20 %)
- Year-by-year growth chart with stacked area visualization — nominal, real, and contributions
- Milestone breakdown at years 1, 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, 30, and 40
- Inflation impact: toggle on/off to see the difference immediately

**Inputs:**

- Initial deposit
- Monthly contribution
- Annual return rate (0–100 %)
- Investment horizon (1–60 years)
- Expected inflation rate (default 3.2 %)
- Annual withdrawal rate for passive income calculation

**Why Investio?**

Most finance apps want your data, your email, or your bank connection. Investio wants nothing. The math runs locally — always. There is no backend, no analytics SDK, no crash reporter phoning home. What you type stays on your phone.

Available in English and Czech. Supports light and dark mode. Respects your device's language on first launch.

**Methodology:** Monthly compounding — monthly rate rₘ = (1+r)^(1/12) − 1, balance B(n+1) = Bₙ · (1+rₘ) + contribution. Real value = nominal / (1+inflation)^years. Passive income = portfolio × annual withdrawal %.
