# Investio — Positioning one-pager

## ICP (Ideal Customer Profile)

Czech retail investor, 25–45 years old, considering their first (or next) investment property while already holding an ETF or stock portfolio. They have a spreadsheet somewhere that does half the job badly. They google "akcie vs nemovitost kalkulačka" and find nothing credible. They are not day-traders. They have a specific decision to make: "Is it better to buy the apartment in Brno, or keep adding to my S&P 500 position?"

Secondary: non-Czech DIY investors in CZ/SK market who want the same comparison in English.

## Problem

Existing tools either:
1. Do compound interest OR rental cashflow — never both, never comparable side-by-side.
2. Are Excel templates from blogs (stale, ugly, hard to share).
3. Come from brokerages with a trading bias built in.

The user can't get a neutral, honest answer to "which investment beats which over MY horizon with MY starting capital."

## Our wedge

Single-purpose comparison tool. Not a brokerage, not a portfolio tracker, not a budgeting app. Just: "here are both scenarios on the same time axis, here is the difference, you decide."

## Core promise

"You can compare an S&P 500 ETF with a Prague apartment on your own numbers in 5 minutes."

## Proof points (available at launch)

- 3 working calculators with live recalculation
- 6 pre-filled templates based on realistic CZ data (S&P 500 1995–2025, Pražský byt průměr 2024, CZ dividendové ETF 2010–2025)
- No account required to try
- Open source (trust through transparency)

## Differentiators vs alternatives

| Alternative | Their gap | Investio advantage |
|---|---|---|
| Finančník.cz / iDnes kalkulačky | Stock-only or real estate-only, no comparison | Side-by-side on same horizon |
| Generic compound interest calculators | No real estate model | Full cashflow: hypotéka + pronájem + daň + údržba |
| Excel templates from blogs | Static, not shareable | Live, URL-shareable scenarios |
| Robo-advisors / brokerages | Conflict of interest | No products to sell, purely educational |

## Brand voice in one sentence

Friendly financial nerd, not a banker and not a crypto bro.

## What we deliberately avoid saying

- "Revolutionary" / "next-generation" / "seamless"
- Anything implying investment advice
- Fake urgency or social proof numbers we don't have
- Claims about returns ("you could earn X%") — we only show what the user inputs

## Tagline candidates (pick one for T-023 hero)

1. "Porovnej akcie s nemovitostmi za 5 minut" — concrete, problem-first, CZ-native
2. "Spočítejte si investici dřív, než ji uděláte" — wireframe version, more advisory tone
3. "Bez excelu. Bez brokera. Jen čísla." — anti-alternative positioning

**Recommendation**: Use #1 as H1. Use #2 as CTA-section heading variant. Use #3 as social media / Product Hunt tagline.
